GoWear Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

GoWear suggests what to wear for a run based on the weather where you are. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: there is no account, no profile, and no server that stores your data. GoWear keeps most of your information only on your phone.

WHAT GOWEAR COLLECTS

Your location. If you allow it, GoWear reads your phone's approximate location while the app is open, to show the weather where you run. It never reads your location in the background and does not send it to us.

Places you type or save. City names and addresses you search for, and the Home, Work and Favorite places you save, are stored on your phone only.

Your choices. Run type, run time, temperature preferences, units and your answers to the run-feedback questions are stored on your phone only.

WHO RECEIVES DATA

Open-Meteo (weather, air quality and city search). To get a forecast, GoWear sends the coordinates of your chosen place, rounded to roughly 1 kilometre, to Open-Meteo. This is required to get the weather and air quality data shown to you.

Apple or Google (place names and addresses). To turn coordinates into a place name such as "Brooklyn, NY", and to find a street address you type, GoWear uses the location services built into your phone.

GoWear does not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools. We do not receive your location or settings on any server of our own.

WHERE YOUR DATA IS STORED

Everything GoWear remembers (settings, saved places, your last forecast and feedback answers) is stored on your phone. It is not uploaded to us and does not sync to a cloud account.

DELETING YOUR DATA

In GoWear, open Settings and choose the option to delete all data. This erases your settings, saved places, cached forecast and feedback answers from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes local app data.

CHILDREN

GoWear is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

HEALTH NOTE

Outfit and timing suggestions are general. They are not medical advice. Listen to your body.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
